# Designated Subjects Career Technical Education (CTE) Teaching Credential

The **Designated Subjects Career Technical Education (CTE) Teaching Credential** authorizes the holder to teach **career technical education** courses in specific industry sectors.

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/cred-type-page.html`, `raw/credential-type-data.txt`, `raw/T01FILE.txt`, `raw/T06FILE.txt`). Downloaded 2026-08-28.

---

## What It Authorizes

> **Source:** CTC authorization statements for Designated Subjects CTE Teaching Credential (`CTE R` / `TC4TR` / `TC4HR`) — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

Per the CTC authorization statements, a CTE credential authorizes the holder to teach in the **subject(s) listed on the credential** in **grades twelve and below** and in **classes organized primarily for adults**, in **career technical instruction courses**.

The credential is issued in a **specific industry sector**. The currently-issued industry sectors include:

- Agriculture and Natural Resources
- Arts, Media, and Entertainment
- Building and Construction Trades
- Business and Finance
- Education, Child Development, and Family Services
- Energy, Environment, and Utilities
- Engineering and Architecture
- Fashion and Interior Design
- Health Science and Medical Technology
- Hospitality, Tourism, and Recreation
- Information and Communication Technology
- Manufacturing and Product Development
- Marketing, Sales, and Service
- Public Service
- Transportation

## Full-Time vs. Part-Time

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

The CTE credential is issued in two forms:

| Form | Code | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| **Full-Time CTE Teaching Credential** | `TC4TR` | Full-time CTE teaching |
| **Part-Time CTE Teaching Credential** | `TC4HR` | Part-time CTE teaching (up to half-time) |

## How It Maps to CALPADS Course Codes

> **Source:** CTC "Appropriate Credentials for CALPADS Course Codes" — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/calsaas/calpads-course-codes/ (raw: `raw/calpads-course-codes.csv`).

A CTE credential **fully authorizes** a course when the course's CALPADS code lists that industry sector as an authorizing credential. For example:

- A **CTE: Agriculture and Natural Resources** credential authorizes agriculture CTE courses (codes `7100`–`7172`).
- A **CTE: Arts, Media, and Entertainment** credential authorizes arts/media CTE courses (codes `7200`–`7263`).
- A **CTE: Building and Construction Trades** credential authorizes building trades CTE courses (codes `7300`+).

**Note:** For many CTE courses, a **Single Subject** credential in a related area (e.g., Agriculture, Business, Home Economics, Industrial and Technology Education) may also provide full authorization, at the employing agency's discretion (Title 5 §80004(c) flexibility).

## Related Documents

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

- **Designated Subjects Adult Education Teaching Credential** (`TC4A`–`TC4E`, `TC4J`) — for adult education settings.
- **Designated Subjects Vocational Education Teaching Credential** (`TC4F`, `TC4G`, `TC4P`, `TC4V`) — legacy vocational education credentials.
- **Designated Subjects Special Subjects Teaching Credential** (`TC4SR`) — for special subjects.
- **30-Day Substitute Designated Subjects CTE Permit** (`SUBER`) — substitute teaching in CTE.

---

## Key Takeaway

A CTE credential = **career technical education in a specific industry sector**. It is the standard credential for CTE/vocational teachers, and maps directly to CTE CALPADS course codes by industry sector.
