# California Teacher Credential Types

This document catalogs the credential, permit, certificate, and authorization types issued by the **California Commission on Teacher Credentialing (CTC)**, based on the official credentialing data files (T01 table) and the Credential Type and Authorization Statements page.

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/cred-type-page.html`, `raw/credential-type-data.txt`, `raw/T01FILE.txt`, `raw/T06FILE.txt`). Downloaded 2026-08-28.

## The Main Teaching Credentials

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

These are the credentials that authorize a person to **teach** in California public schools.

### Multiple Subject Teaching Credential
- **Codes:** `TC2 R`, `PTC2R`, `TCMSR`
- **What it authorizes:** Teaching in **self-contained classrooms** — typically elementary grades (TK–6) and some middle school settings where one teacher teaches all/most subjects to one class.
- **Key detail:** The holder teaches "multiple subjects" (all core subjects) to a single group of students, rather than one subject to many groups.
- **CALPADS link:** Fully authorizes course code `1000` (Self-Contained Class).

### Single Subject Teaching Credential
- **Codes:** `TC1 R`, `TCSSR`
- **What it authorizes:** Teaching a **specific subject** (e.g., Mathematics, English, Science, History–Social Science, Art, Music, Physical Education, World Languages) in **departmentalized settings** — typically middle and high school (grades 7–12).
- **Key detail:** The credential is issued in a specific subject area; the holder teaches that subject to multiple groups of students.
- **CALPADS link:** Authorizes departmentalized courses in the holder's subject area.

### Education Specialist Instruction Credential
- **Codes:** `TC3SR`, `TC3HR` (older), `TC3QR` (permit)
- **What it authorizes:** Teaching **students with disabilities** (special education). Includes specific disability-area authorizations (see [Special Education Authorizations](special-ed-authorizations.md)).
- **Key detail:** Modern credentials are issued in support-need categories: Mild/Moderate Support Needs, Extensive Support Needs, Deaf and Hard of Hearing, Early Childhood Special Education, Visual Impairments.

### Designated Subjects Career Technical Education (CTE) Teaching Credential
- **Codes:** `CTE R`, `TC4TR` (full-time), `TC4HR` (part-time)
- **What it authorizes:** Teaching **career technical education** courses in specific industry sectors (e.g., Agriculture, Arts/Media/Entertainment, Building Trades, Health Science, Information Technology).
- **Key detail:** Authorizes teaching in grades twelve and below and in classes organized primarily for adults, in career technical instruction courses.

### Specialist Instruction Credentials

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

- **Codes:** `TC3A`–`TC3H` (Agriculture, Bilingual Crosscultural, Early Childhood, Gifted, Health Science, Mathematics, Reading, Special Education)
- **What they authorize:** Teaching in a specialized instructional area, often as a specialist/consultant rather than a general classroom teacher.

## Permits

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

Permits are limited-term documents that authorize specific, often temporary, service.

| Permit | Code | Purpose |
|--------|------|---------|
| **30-Day Substitute Teaching Permit** | `SUBPR` | Substitute teaching for up to 30 days |
| **Career Substitute Teaching Permit** | `SUBCR` | Substitute teaching (longer-term) |
| **Substitute Teaching Permit for Prospective Teachers** | `SUBTR` | Substitute teaching while pursuing a credential |
| **Short-Term Staff Permit** | `TC13R` | Emergency staffing for up to one year |
| **Provisional Internship Permit** | `TC14R` | Provisional intern teaching |
| **District Intern Credential** | `TC10R` | Intern teaching through a district program |
| **Pre-Intern Certificate** | `TC11R` | Pre-intern status |
| **Teaching Permit for Statutory Leave** | `TPSLR` | Teaching during a statutory leave |
| **Limited Assignment Authorization** | `TC1LR` | Authorizes a specific limited assignment |
| **General Education Limited Assignment Permits** | `TLA1R`/`TLA2R` | Limited assignment in single/multiple subject |
| **Special Education Limited Assignment Permit** | `TLA3R` | Limited assignment in special education |
| **Visiting Faculty Permit** | `VFP R` | Visiting faculty teaching |
| **Child Development Permits** | `P1`–`P12` | Child development program service |

## Added Authorizations

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

Added authorizations (AA) are added to a base credential to expand what the holder may do.

| Added Authorization | Code | Expands to |
|---------------------|------|------------|
| **Reading and Literacy Added Authorization** | `RLAA` | Reading/literacy instruction |
| **Bilingual Authorization** | `SA15R` | Bilingual instruction |
| **Resource Specialist Added Authorization** | `SA1R` | Resource specialist service |
| **Early Childhood Special Education Added Authorization** | `SA2R` | Early childhood special ed |
| **Adapted Physical Education Added Authorization** | `SA7R` | Adapted PE |
| **Autism Spectrum Disorders Added Authorization** | `AAAS` | Autism instruction |
| **Deaf-Blind Added Authorization** | `AADB` | Deaf-blind instruction |
| **Emotional Disturbance Added Authorization** | `AAED` | Emotional disturbance |
| **Orthopedic Impairment Added Authorization** | `AAOI` | Orthopedic impairment |
| **Other Health Impairment Added Authorization** | `AAOH` | Other health impairment |
| **Traumatic Brain Injury Added Authorization** | `AATB` | Traumatic brain injury |
| **Early Childhood Added Authorization** | `ECSE` | Early childhood |
| **Resource Specialist Added Authorization** | `RSAA` | Resource specialist |
| **Adapted Physical Education Added Authorization** | `APEA` | Adapted PE |

## Services Credentials (Non-Teaching)

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

These authorize **non-teaching** professional services in schools:

| Credential | Code | Authorizes |
|------------|------|------------|
| **Administrative Services Credential** | `SC1AR` | Principal, vice principal, administrator |
| **Pupil Personnel Services Credential** | `SC5R` | School counselor, psychologist, social worker |
| **School Nurse Services Credential** | `SC3AR` | School nursing |
| **Health Services Credential** | `SC3R` | Health services |
| **Clinical or Rehabilitative Services Credential** | `SC4R` | Speech/language, audiology, etc. |
| **Librarianship Services Credential** | `SC2R` | School librarian |
| **Library Media Teacher Services Credential** | `SC2AR` | Library media teacher |
| **Teacher Librarian Services Credential** | `SC2BR` | Teacher librarian |
| **General Child Welfare and Supervision of Attendance Credential** | `GS1G` | Child welfare/attendance officer |

## Legacy / Historical Credentials

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`, `raw/T01FILE.txt`).

The data includes many **legacy credentials** no longer issued but still held by some educators (e.g., General Teaching Credentials `GT1`–`GT7`, Standard Secondary Teaching Credential `STCB`, Standard Designated Subjects credentials `SDTC`/`SDTG`/`SDTH`). These are documented in the raw T01/T06 data files for completeness.
