# Multiple Subject Teaching Credential

The **Multiple Subject Teaching Credential** authorizes the holder to teach in **self-contained classrooms** — typically elementary grades (TK–6) and some middle school settings where one teacher teaches all/most core subjects to a single class.

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/cred-type-page.html`, `raw/credential-type-data.txt`, `raw/T01FILE.txt`, `raw/T06FILE.txt`). Downloaded 2026-08-28.

---

## What It Authorizes

> **Source:** CTC authorization statements for Multiple Subject Teaching Credential (`TC2 R` / `TCMSR`) — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

Per the CTC authorization statements, a Multiple Subject Teaching Credential authorizes the holder to teach **multiple subjects** (all core subjects) in **self-contained classrooms**. The holder teaches a single group of students across the curriculum, rather than one subject to many groups.

**Core subjects covered** in a self-contained classroom typically include:
- English / Language Arts
- Mathematics
- Science
- History–Social Science
- Health
- Visual and Performing Arts
- Physical Education

---

## Grade-Level Authorization

> **Source:** CTC authorization statements for Multiple Subject Teaching Credential — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

The standard Multiple Subject credential authorizes teaching in **grades TK–6** (transitional kindergarten through sixth grade), and in some configurations extends to **grade 8** in self-contained settings. The exact range depends on the specific document.

---

## How It Maps to CALPADS Course Codes

> **Source:** CTC "Appropriate Credentials for CALPADS Course Codes" — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/calsaas/calpads-course-codes/ (raw: `raw/calpads-course-codes.csv`).

A Multiple Subject credential **fully authorizes** course code `1000` (Self-Contained Class). This is the primary CALPADS code for elementary self-contained classrooms.

**Important:** A Multiple Subject credential does **not** authorize departmentalized single-subject teaching at the secondary level (that requires a Single Subject credential).

## Related Documents

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

- **Multiple Subject Teaching Permit** (`TC2PR`) — a permit version for limited circumstances.
- **Supplementary Authorization** (`TC2SR`) — added to a Multiple Subject credential to authorize teaching an additional subject.
- **Limited Assignment Authorization** (`TC1LR`) — authorizes a specific limited assignment.
- **PK-3 Early Childhood Education Specialist Instruction Credential** (`PK3 R`) — for early childhood (PK–3) settings.

---

## Key Takeaway

A Multiple Subject credential = **many subjects, one class** (self-contained). It is the standard credential for elementary classroom teachers.
