# Education Specialist Instruction Credential

The **Education Specialist Instruction Credential** authorizes the holder to teach **students with disabilities** (special education) in California public schools.

---

## What It Authorizes

An Education Specialist credential authorizes the holder to provide **special education instruction and services** to students with disabilities. The specific disability categories and grade levels a holder may serve depend on the **authorization** printed on the credential.

---

## Modern Authorization Categories

Modern Education Specialist credentials are issued in **support-need categories**:

| Authorization | Code | Grade Level | Student Support Level |
|---------------|------|-------------|------------------------|
| **Mild/Moderate Support Needs** | `R3MM` | TK–12 (up to age 22), Adult Ed | Mild to Moderate only |
| **Extensive Support Needs** | `R3MS` | TK–12 (up to age 22), Adult Ed | Extensive only |
| **Deaf and Hard of Hearing** | `R3DH` | Birth–age 22, Adult Ed | All |
| **Early Childhood Special Education** | `R3EC` | Birth–Pre-K | All |
| **Visual Impairments** | `R3VI` | Birth–age 22, Adult Ed | All |
| **Physical & Health Impairment** | `R3PI` | Birth–age 22, Adult Ed | All |
| **Language & Academic Development** | `R3LD` | Pre-K–age 22 | All |

---

## Bridge Authorizations

Bridge authorizations allow a holder of one support-need category to serve students in another category for disabilities not covered by their base authorization:

| Bridge Authorization | Code | Grade Level | Covers |
|----------------------|------|-------------|--------|
| **Education Specialist Bridge – Mild to Moderate Support Needs** | `R3BM` | TK–12 (up to age 22), Adult Ed | Mild to Moderate only, for disabilities unauthorized by R3MM |
| **Education Specialist Bridge – Extensive Support Needs** | `R3BE` | TK–12 (up to age 22), Adult Ed | Extensive only, for disabilities unauthorized by R3MS |
| **Education Specialist Bridge – Early Childhood** | `R3BC` | Birth–K | All |

---

## Added Authorizations (Disability-Specific)

Added authorizations (AA) expand a base special education credential to cover specific disability categories:

| Added Authorization | Code | Covers |
|---------------------|------|--------|
| **Autism Spectrum Disorders** | `AAAS` | Autism |
| **Deaf-Blind** | `AADB` | Deaf-blind |
| **Emotional Disturbance** | `AAED` | Emotional disturbance |
| **Orthopedic Impairment** | `AAOI` | Orthopedic impairment |
| **Other Health Impairment** | `AAOH` | Other health impairment |
| **Traumatic Brain Injury** | `AATB` | Traumatic brain injury |
| **Early Childhood** | `ECSE` | Early childhood |
| **Resource Specialist** | `RSAA` | Resource specialist service |
| **Adapted Physical Education** | `APEA` | Adapted PE |

---

## Key Takeaway

An Education Specialist credential = **special education teaching**, with the specific disability categories and grade levels determined by the authorization on the document. Modern credentials are organized by **support-need category** (Mild/Moderate, Extensive, Deaf/Hard of Hearing, Early Childhood, Visual Impairments).
