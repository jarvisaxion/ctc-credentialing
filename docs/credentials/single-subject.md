# Single Subject Teaching Credential

The **Single Subject Teaching Credential** authorizes the holder to teach a **specific subject** in **departmentalized settings** — typically middle and high school (grades 7–12).

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/cred-type-page.html`, `raw/credential-type-data.txt`, `raw/T01FILE.txt`, `raw/T06FILE.txt`). Downloaded 2026-08-28.

---

## What It Authorizes

> **Source:** CTC authorization statements for Single Subject Teaching Credential (`TC1 R` / `TCSSR`) — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

Per the CTC authorization statements, a Single Subject Teaching Credential authorizes the holder to teach the **subject(s) listed on the credential** in **grades seven through twelve** (and, in some configurations, grades K–12 depending on the subject and the specific document).

The credential is issued in a **specific subject area**. Common subject areas include:

- Mathematics
- English / Language Arts
- Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geosciences, General Science)
- History–Social Science
- Art
- Music
- Physical Education
- World Languages (Spanish, French, etc.)
- Agriculture
- Business
- Home Economics
- Industrial and Technology Education

---

## Grade-Level Authorization

> **Source:** CTC authorization statements for Single Subject Teaching Credential — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

The standard Single Subject credential authorizes teaching in **grades 7–12**. However, the actual grade range depends on the specific document and subject:

- **Standard:** grades 7–12 (departmentalized secondary settings)
- **Some subjects/documents:** may extend to grades K–12 (e.g., Art, Music, Physical Education, World Languages can authorize K–12 in certain configurations)
- **Adult education:** classes organized primarily for adults are generally authorized

## How It Maps to CALPADS Course Codes

> **Source:** CTC "Appropriate Credentials for CALPADS Course Codes" — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/calsaas/calpads-course-codes/ (raw: `raw/calpads-course-codes.csv`).

A Single Subject credential **fully authorizes** a course when the course's CALPADS code lists that subject as an authorizing credential. For example:

- A **Single Subject: Mathematics** credential authorizes departmentalized mathematics courses.
- A **Single Subject: Science** credential authorizes departmentalized science courses.
- A **Single Subject: Art** credential authorizes art courses.

**Important:** A Single Subject credential does **not** authorize self-contained (multiple subject) classes. Course code `1000` (Self-Contained Class) requires a **Multiple Subject** credential.

## Related Documents

> **Source:** CTC Credential Type and Authorization Statements page — https://www.ctc.ca.gov/employers/tools-and-resources/credential-type-and-authorization-statements/ (raw: `raw/credential-type-data.txt`).

- **Single Subject Teaching Permit** (`TC1PR`) — a permit version for limited circumstances.
- **Supplementary Authorization** (`TC1SR`) — added to a Single Subject credential to authorize teaching an additional subject.
- **Limited Assignment Authorization** (`TC1LR`) — authorizes a specific limited assignment.
- **Visiting Faculty Single Subject Teaching Credential** (`VFC R`) — for visiting faculty.

---

## Key Takeaway

A Single Subject credential = **one subject, many classes** (departmentalized). It is the standard credential for middle and high school subject-area teachers.
