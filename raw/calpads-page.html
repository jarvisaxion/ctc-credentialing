Appropriate Credentials for CALPADS Course Codes

The table below is intended to support credential analysts in determining which authorizations are required to teach in course assignments. This table includes currently issued documents, and may not reflect previously issued documents.

It is important to note, some documents listed have limited authorizations. Refer to the key below for guidance:

  • Supplementary Authorization: Limited to students in grade 9 and below, not limited by curriculum level.
  • Specific Supplementary Authorization: Limited to specific subject area listed on the document
  • Specific Subject Matter Authorization: Limited to specific subject area listed on the document
  • Introductory Supplementary Authorization: Limited to Curriculum for grades 9 and below
  • Introductory Subject Matter Authorization: Limited to Curriculum for grades 9 and below

Credentials and authorization codes issued by the Commission can be found in the Authorization Sort Table.
