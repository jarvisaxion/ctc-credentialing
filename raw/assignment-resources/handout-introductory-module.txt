     Career Technical Education (CTE) Setting

     ▪Curriculum designated as technical, trade, or
     vocational

     ▪50% or more of the course content based on the CTE
     Model Curriculum Standards

       Child Development Permits
       ▪For use in child care and development programs
       ▪Designed for individuals working with children ages 5
       and below
       ▪Multiple levels that authorize different levels of care
       and instruction

     Local Assignment Options
     ▪Otherwise legally authorize a fully credentialed
     educator to serve in an assignment outside of the
     area authorized by their credential
     ▪Fully determined at the local level
     ▪May require governing board action
     ▪Teacher consent is required
