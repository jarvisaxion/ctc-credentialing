     Special Education Transitional Kindergarten
    Special Education credentials
    authorize the holder to teach any
    academic content in a special
    education classroom (including TK) if
    the authorization aligns with:
      Disability area(s) of the students
      Grades/Ages of the students
      Level of support needs of the
       students

     English Learners (ELs) in TK Classrooms
       If one or more students in the
        class are designated as requiring
        EL services, then the educator
        must provide EL services
       Educator must hold an EL
        authorization that aligns with
        the required level of EL services
