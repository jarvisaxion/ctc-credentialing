      What is a Self-Contained Setting?
        The same group of students are taught multiple
         subjects by one educator throughout the day.

                           Regrouping
    ▪When two or more teachers each teach all subjects in
    a self-contained classroom, but group their students
    for one or more of those subjects. Students may be
    grouped by their learning level.

                                     Team Teaching
        Team teaching occurs when two teachers divide their self-contained classroom
        instruction based on each teacher’s strengths in a specific subject area(s). For
        example, one teacher might provide instruction in English language arts to both
        classes (at different times)
                    Mr. Fitzgerald (Group A)                               Mrs. Edison (Group B)

                   Subject 4: Lunch                                   Subject 4: Lunch
                   Subject 5: Prep                                    Subject 5: Social Science
                   Subject 6: Social Science                          Subject 6: Math
                   Subject 7: Math                                    Subject 7: Prep

                     Middle School Core Setting
                                                                    A core setting is one in which a teacher is
                                                                    assigned on the basis of a credential that
                                                                    authorizes service in a self-contained
                                                                    classroom and:
                                                                    1. Teaches two or more subjects to the
                                                                       same group of students in the same
                                                                       day, and;
                                                                    2. The student population for the
                                                                       class(es) remains substantially the
                                                                       same.

     Middle School Core Setting: Hanging Period
     ▪Same subject already being taught in core assignment
     ▪Same grade level as core assignment
     ▪Cannot constitute more than half of the teacher’s
     total assignment

13




            What courses can be “cored”?
     ▪Can be Academic or Elective
     ▪Must be grade 5-8

     ▪Not authorized to be part of a core setting:
      • Departmentalized ELD
      • Career Technical Education (CTE) courses

               Transitional Kindergarten (TK)
     ▪First year of a 2 year kindergarten
      program
     ▪Require a Multiple Subject credential
     Currently issued documents that
     authorize teaching TK:
     ▪Full Multiple Subject Credential, University
      Intern, District Intern, GELAP, STSP, PIP




                                                                                                 29



29




               Transitional Kindergarten (TK)
     EC section 48000(g) requires credentialed teachers who are
     first assigned to a TK classroom after July 1, 2015 to have
     one of the following by August 1, 2021:
     ▪24+ units in early childhood education, or childhood
      development, or both
     ▪Professional experience in a classroom setting with
      preschool age children comparable to the 24 units of
      education described in bullet 1
     ▪Child Development Teacher Permit
