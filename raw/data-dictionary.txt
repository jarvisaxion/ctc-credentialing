MEMORANDUM

DATE:         June 2015

TO:           County Office of Education Credential Analysts and Technical Support Staff

SUBJECT:      Weekly Document Data Files for County Offices of Education; New Format Phase-In

CTC is phasing in a slight change to the weekly exchange file. Entities that receive data through CSIS will receive
files in the new format; they may request through CSIS the obsolete file format. COEs receiving data from CTC will
receive both formats through December 2015 to allow them time to update their systems.


Each week the Commission creates files containing document information using the county of employment field.
The county offices generally use this information to update their records for assignment and payroll purposes. The
authority for this sharing of credential information can be found in Education Code §44230.

The Commission creates the following data files and the county office may receive any and all formats:
    • a pdf file that contains the documents that were issued for that week.
    • an electronic file containing the document information in a fixed-length format.
    • an electronic file contain the document information in a tab-delimited format.

Electronic files are placed on the Commission’s SFTP server hosted by OTECH; the SFTP server encrypts the data
both in transmission and while at rest. Counties maintain their passwords to access their SFTP directory in
accordance with OTECH requirements. For additional security the data is encrypted using PGP software and a
different password from their SFTP access.

Attached is the:
    • list of the data that the Commission includes in the electronic document information file.
    • data dictionary that defines each of the fields.

Contact Information:

Contact the CTC Help Desk, hdesk@ctc.ca.gov

RESTR-CODE-1

   FIELD TYPE:   Alphanumeric
   FIELD LENGTH: 1, Position 412 through 412

   DEFINITION:
   NOTES: Descriptions of the values:
          2 = restricted to the county schools
             “Restricted to service in the schools under the jurisdiction (Name of County)”
          3 = restricted to a non-public school in a county – Issue date BEFORE 7/1/1995
             “Restricted to service in a non-public school located in (Name of County)”
             or restricted to a non-public school – Issue date AFTER 7/1/1995
             “Restricted to service in the following non-public school or agency: (Name of NPS/NPA)”
          4 = restricted to an IHE and nonpublic school or agency for an internship
             “Restricted to service under the joint supervision of (Name of IHE) and (Name of NPS/NPA),
             (Name of County)”
          5 = restricted to an IHE and district or county for an internship
             “Restricted to service under the joint supervision of (Name of IHE) and (Name of District), (Name
             of County)”
          6 = restricted to the county and district or to an agency or charter school
             “Restricted to service in (Name of School District or Charter School), (Name of County)”
             or
             “Restricted to service in (Name of Agency or Charter School)”
